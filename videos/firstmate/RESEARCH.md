# Firstmate — research and creative contract

Researched 2026-09-11. Primary repository: [kunchenguid/firstmate](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate), inspected at `31f062d43d25775f8010480cbcba56134aac1998`. Research clone was read-only; no Firstmate agents or scripts were launched.

## Product truth

- [README](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate/blob/31f062d43d25775f8010480cbcba56134aac1998/README.md): an agent distro, not another model, harness, hosted app, MCP server or CLI. Clone the directory and launch a supported coding agent inside it. One first mate delegates and supervises crewmates, with isolated worktrees and visible sessions; outputs include PRs, authorized local merges or investigation reports.
- [Vision](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate/blob/31f062d43d25775f8010480cbcba56134aac1998/VISION.md): its central benefit is less attention spent coordinating work. Outcomes and consequential decisions reach the captain; internal chatter does not. Authority is explicit, and durable records make restart/reconciliation possible. Do not turn this into an unqualified promise that every task succeeds or merges automatically.
- [Architecture](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate/blob/31f062d43d25775f8010480cbcba56134aac1998/docs/architecture.md): supervision is event-driven. The shell watcher does mechanical waiting, with agents involved when actionable events arise. Avoid claiming the whole product uses zero tokens.
- [Relay site](https://myfirstmate.io/): optional X/Discord bridge to the user's own local Firstmate. Linked-owner routing, connector tokens and request-bound replies; not a public bot anyone can command. The film labels this optional and owner-only.
- [Calm mode](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate/blob/31f062d43d25775f8010480cbcba56134aac1998/docs/calm.md): Pi-specific presentation with a small moving boat; not a universal dashboard. This supports the nautical metaphor, not a claim that our animated chart is shipped UI.
