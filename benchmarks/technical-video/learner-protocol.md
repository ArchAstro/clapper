# Learner pilot protocol — human phase, not completed

No participants have been recruited and no learning outcomes have been measured.
This document is the preregistration template for E07, not evidence of improvement.

1. **Participants:** 24 target-audience viewers, assigned randomly to baseline or
   candidate (12 each). Obtain consent and use anonymous IDs. Record prerequisite
   knowledge using a short screen; do not substitute LLM personas for people.
2. **Exposure:** three distinct topic families per participant, balanced across arms.
   No person sees both versions of the same topic. Keep video time, environment,
   playback affordances and access to notes equal. Log playback/skips separately.
3. **Assessments:** prepare equivalent pre/post forms before evaluating candidates.
   Include factual recall, causal explanation and a novel transfer problem. Keep
   answer keys and scoring criteria outside author/optimizer workspaces. Randomize
   form order where appropriate. State whether the posttest is immediate or delayed.
4. **Primary outcome:** preregister the transfer-score definition and a minimum
   practically worthwhile gain. Use blinded scoring, with a second scorer on a
   sample and a rule for resolving disagreements. Report recall separately.
5. **Analysis:** use pretest-adjusted arm comparisons; preserve participant and topic
   dependence (72 viewings are not 72 independent participants). Report missingness,
   exclusions decided before outcomes, uncertainty, and every preregistered metric.
6. **Purpose of pilot:** estimate feasibility and variance. Use those estimates for
   a power calculation before a confirmatory claim. A positive point estimate or
   an LLM preference does not establish better learning.
7. **Later controls:** compare matched transcript/static-diagram and video versions
   to isolate what motion and narration contribute. This is a separate experiment,
   not an extra unplanned arm added after seeing results.

Required preregistration fields: comparison/movie hashes, audience definition,
recruitment/consent approach, randomization seed and allocation, forms and answer-key
hashes, outcome/scoring rules, analysis method, exclusions, sample size rationale,
reviewer identities, and the decision to expand/stop. Do not auto-promote a skill
based on a simulated cohort or unpowered pilot.
