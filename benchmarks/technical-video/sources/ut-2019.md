# Universal Transformers, ICLR 2019, §2.1–2.2

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03819v3

Pack type: curator-written reference notes, not a verbatim copy of the primary publication. Freeze/approve these notes before confirmatory evaluation.

Universal Transformers repeatedly revise token representations across depth, with parameters shared across revisions. Token positions are processed in parallel at each revision.
The original architecture has an encoder and an autoregressive decoder with separate parameter sets. The encoder processes input embeddings; the decoder consumes an available output prefix with masked self-attention and cross-attention to the final encoder states.
Vocabulary projection and softmax produce next-token probabilities. Token selection and appending to the prefix belong to the outer generation loop, not the internal depth loop.
Position and depth signals are added each revision. Attention and a transition have residual connections and normalization. The transition may be a position-wise affine–ReLU–affine network or separable convolution. A diagram of the encoder alone must say so.
An optional ACT variant permits per-position halting. A fixed-depth backpropagation explanation must not be advertised as deriving all adaptive-halting gradients.
Do not substitute Looped Transformers as Programmable Computers (Giannou et al.2023) or Huginn2025. Story examples are illustrations unless an actual measured trace is supplied.
