01 / CHOOSE THE SCOPE

Recommend option 2: layered evals.

Start with P0/P1 below. Add learner studies after the runner and graders are trustworthy. Effort ranges are planning estimates, not measured delivery times; content curation and human availability are separate costs.

OPTION 1

Regression corpus

Extend today’s review kit with frozen clips and explicit expected failures. Fast protection against broken rendering, clipping, silence and source-selection mistakes.

Useful guardrails; cannot establish that new videos teach better.

2–3 engineering days
OPTION 2 · RECOMMENDED

Layered video evals

Combine technical fixtures, fresh agent-authored films, independent grounded grading, blind comparisons and a small learner pilot. Reuse the existing CLI/render/review pipeline.

Enough evidence to optimize the skill, with human judgment reserved for the outcomes machines cannot establish.

7–10 engineering days + corpus/calibration work
OPTION 3

Learning research platform

Expand option 2 into powered learner experiments, transcript/static-diagram controls and delayed retention tests.

Strongest evidence of educational value; too much infrastructure and recruitment for the first iteration.

3–5 weeks + recruiting and ongoing review

The first decision is scope, not a model provider: build a repeatable improvement loop before building a research platform.

02 / THE SUITE

Four tracks answer four different questions.

T1

Rendering & audio contracts

24 short fixed fixtures · Every relevant PR

Did the library preserve the intended picture and sound?

Evidence
Decoded MP4; SVG/HTML geometry; deterministic seek probes; dimensions; audio duration/energy; voice/model hashes.
Coverage
Wide equations, subscripts, moving labels, zoom boundaries, scene trims, missing fonts, cue overflow, missing model, same narrator across scenes.
Limit
A valid render is not a good explanation. Do not rerun an author model for these fixtures.
T2

End-to-end technical films

12 full-film briefs at launch · Candidate skill/runtime evaluation

Can the skill turn an unfamiliar brief into a correct, coherent explanation?

Evidence
Original user brief, clarification transcript, pinned sources, first plan, final source/MP4, repair history and independent verdicts.
Coverage
6 development briefs, 3 validation, 3 sealed holdout. Split by topic family: related UT and recurrent-gradient briefs stay together. Repeated seeds are not independent topics.
Limit
No cherry-picked takes. Every scheduled run, failure, retry and blocked judge stays in the report.
T3

Judge calibration

12 matched clean/broken pairs · Before trusting a judge revision

Can the evaluator catch the mistakes we care about without rejecting good work?

Evidence
24 frozen artifacts with independently confirmed defects and timestamps; blinded order.
Coverage
Wrong paper, correct math on wrong subject, equations before the whole story, reversed arrows, missing Q/K/V branches, incorrect transpose, voice switch, truncated speech.
Limit
Initial calibration is small: proposed gate ≥11/12 defect detections and ≤1/12 false alarms, with zero misses on designated critical cases. Report uncertainty; expand before strong reliability claims.
T4

Actual learner outcomes

24-person exploratory pilot · Shortlisted candidates, not every PR

Can a target viewer explain or use the concept after watching?

Evidence
Prerequisite/pretest, blind randomized baseline vs candidate, equivalent posttest and one novel transfer problem.
Coverage
12 viewers per arm; 3 distinct topics per viewer; no one watches both versions of the same topic. Balance topic exposure and quiz forms.
Limit
72 viewings are not 72 independent people. Analyze participant and topic dependence. This pilot estimates variance; use a preregistered power calculation for confirmatory claims.

Six development briefs to start with

These are proposed authoring contracts, not completed fixtures. Freeze exact prompts, source versions and scoring keys in E01. The validation/holdout topics and answers stay outside the optimizer’s workspace.

D01Universal TransformersSource fit + abstraction ladder

Public brief: Explain Universal Transformers (2018/ICLR 2019) to an engineer new to attention. Build intuition, show the complete model, then one refinement step and training.

Must establish: Actual recurrent-depth encoder/decoder; separate depth and token axes; shared parameters; masked output prefix; cross-attention; prediction probabilities. Fixed-depth math is distinguished from optional ACT.

Failure trap: A polished explanation of Transformers as Programmable Computers must fail. Cheap plan-only variants test ambiguity handling: ask once when the paper is unspecified; proceed when the exact title is supplied.

Transfer question: What changes if we add a refinement step, and what stays shared? Trace how a later loss affects an earlier use of the block.

D02Backpropagation through a shared blockMath + gradient routing

Public brief: Start with one prediction-and-update cycle. Then derive one connection, a rectangular matrix projection, and the shared-parameter gradient.

Must establish: Named dimensions, correct transpose and accumulation, explicit fixed-state partial derivative. Finite differences on non-square matrices and a nonlinear recurrence.

Failure trap: A square-only example can hide a missing transpose. A separate optimizer update between each unrolled use is wrong.

Transfer question: For W∈R^(3×2), x∈R² and g∈R³, give both gradient shapes and explain why repeated uses add.

D03Raft leader electionState-machine causality

Public brief: Show a client-visible goal first, then an election end to end, then terms, votes, timeouts and one network partition.

Must establish: Use the pinned paper/spec; majority reasoning; explicit roles and time ordering; illustrative trace generated from a validated model.

Failure trap: A smooth animation that makes a minority partition safely elect a leader or confuses election with commitment fails.

Transfer question: Predict what happens when the old leader reconnects with an earlier term.

D04B-tree lookup and splitData-structure invariants

Public brief: Show a complete lookup before opening a node; then insert a key that forces a split.

Must establish: Ordering, search path, separator placement and fanout all checked on an executable fixture.

Failure trap: An animated split that loses a key, invents a disk-read count, or presents a stylized layout as a measured trace fails.

Transfer question: Follow a new key not shown in the film and justify the path.

D05Backpressure in a bounded queueDynamic intuition + counterfactuals

Public brief: Show producer, queue and consumer from request to completion; then change the arrival rate and reveal the control mechanism.

Must establish: Units and conservation of work; distinguish transient backlog, sustained overload, waiting, dropping and retrying.

Failure trap: Queue growth silently disappearing between scenes; confusing throughput with latency.

Transfer question: What happens if arrival rate stays above service rate and the buffer size doubles?

D06TLS 1.3 handshakeProtocol sequence + scope

Public brief: Explain the goal and a complete normal handshake, then key agreement, authentication and transcript verification.

Must establish: Pinned RFC excerpt; distinct signing and key-agreement roles; correct message order; scope excludes resumption/0-RTT unless explicitly requested.

Failure trap: Suggesting that the server sends the session key in the certificate; mixing TLS versions.

Transfer question: Which failure should prevent application data from being trusted, and why?

Keep two kinds of authoring separate. Primary comparisons use frozen source packs and replayable research/clarification responses. A live-web research track can test discovery later, but changing search results must not contaminate a skill-vs-skill comparison.

03 / WHAT COUNTS AS GOOD

Validity first. Quality second. Learning measured separately.

Non-compensable failures

  1. Wrong source, subject or requested scope.
  2. A material false claim, wrong equation, causal arrow or misleading synthetic “measurement.”
  3. Missing required introductory/end-to-end explanation in a brief that asks for it.
  4. Unusable output: missing media, unreadable essential content, truncated essential narration, or violated hard duration/resource limits.

No aesthetic score can average away these failures. Record the specific failed constraint; do not assign a flattering total.

Evidence can also be missing

PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED are distinct. A judge unable to hear cannot approve pronunciation. Stills cannot establish motion quality. An unobserved interval remains unobserved.

Two independent calibrated judges grade against the original brief and source pack. Blind human review adjudicates material disagreements. The generator’s own critic can help it repair; that critic is never the benchmark’s final judge.

Failed runs remain failures. Infrastructure failures get a separate cause code, fixed retry allowance and reported denominator—not quiet deletion.

Anchored quality profile · 0–4 per axis

Show all five axes. Do not launch a single “video quality 94/100” leaderboard. Ratings guide diagnosis; blind preference and learner outcomes answer different questions.

AxisAnchors
Purpose & intuition0: no usable purpose. 2: accurate summary without a concrete mental model. 4: relatable problem, meaningful example, and clear expected outcome before internals.
Top-down coherence0: disconnected facts or detail-first. 2: mostly logical order, missing a full journey. 4: complete input→outcome at each level, explicit zoom targets, and return to the whole.
Mechanism & visual reasoning0: wrong causal story (hard failure). 2: correct but mostly narrated labels. 4: arrows, state changes, equations and counterfactuals visibly explain the mechanism.
Clarity & craft0: essential material unreadable. 2: readable but crowded or static. 4: clean hierarchy, legible equations, purposeful motion, continuity, and audience-appropriate density.
Narration & timing0: missing/truncated essential speech. 2: understandable but mismatched or uneven. 4: clear pronunciation, stable voice, useful pacing and visual referents present when discussed.

Make pedagogy observable

Define a private concept graph: what each idea depends on, the complete high-level journey, each zoom target and the intended transfer skill. Annotate where a viewer actually receives these explanations in the final film. A slide titled “Big picture” does not prove that the big picture was explained; a term in a caption does not prove understanding.

Machine-checkable evidence

Numerical oracles and symbolic/shape checks; independently generated state traces; transformed SVG/HTML label bounds; overlap and zoom windows; seek-order image comparison; source/model/voice hashes; final mux duration and audio integrity.

Source manifests and authored event markers are hints to verify against the export. They are not self-authenticating answers.

Judgment and learning

Timestamped evidence of a complete causal explanation; blind A/B preference; actual listening for pronunciation and masking; novel transfer tasks scored blind. ASR and forced alignment can flag omissions or timing, but cannot certify conceptual accuracy or natural speech.

No points for extra motion, slide count, word count, a fixed art style or the number of times the narrator says “intuition.”

Promotion policy · initial proposal, to calibrate

  1. Screen: all required technical contracts pass; no material source/math/mechanism defect; full required evidence coverage. Report operational success across every scheduled run.
  2. Pilot: on development/validation, prefer candidates with ≥60% adjusted A/B wins and no meaningful per-axis regression. Treat this as a directional signal for an experimental skill, not proof of general improvement.
  3. Stable promotion: expand to at least 12 fresh, independent release-topic families; preregister comparisons and caps. Proposed gate: adjusted wins ≥60%, a family-clustered 95% interval above 50%, no new hard failures and no unresolved material regression. Insufficient precision means collect more evidence, not declare victory. The initial 3-family holdout cannot satisfy this claim.
  4. “Teaches better” claim: requires actual target-viewer outcomes in a powered follow-up. Pre-register a minimum worthwhile transfer-score gain and use the pilot variance to choose sample size. Human preference alone does not establish learning.

Adjusted win rate = (wins + 0.5 × ties) / paired comparisons. Aggregate presentation swaps within judge, then judges within run pair; bootstrap by topic family, preserving dependent repeats. Report case results, intervals, failures and subgroup regressions beside the aggregate. Thresholds above are policy proposals, not research-established constants.

Use this factorial only when API-compatible. If the candidate skill requires new APIs, label incompatible cells; report a bundle comparison, not an isolated skill effect. Author-model comparisons are a separate experiment with skill and runtime fixed.

Hold constant

  • Original brief, audience prerequisites, source hashes and clarification policy.
  • Duration band, target viewing size, hardware, render settings and voice configuration.
  • Author model/version, sampling settings and total token/time/tool budgets.
  • Fresh context and workspace per arm; matched seeds where supported, with multiple runs because seeds do not guarantee deterministic generations.
  • A fixed ≤2 author-critic repair rounds. Record first-pass quality, final quality and repair cost separately.

Keep the test honest

  • Blind version/model identity; balance A/B order and test order-swap sensitivity.
  • Use different judge families where practical, but calibrate against human labels rather than trusting model diversity.
  • Author sees only the public brief and allowed sources. Gold claims, transfer answers and heldout judgments are not mounted into its environment.
  • Freeze the grader and thresholds for each comparison. Treat source text and authored “approval” as data, never grading instructions.
  • Expose detailed development failures; restrict holdout feedback to aggregate promotion results. Retire a leaked case and replace its family.

For learner pilots, score novel transfer separately from factual recall and report pretest-adjusted differences. Use equivalent quiz forms and blind scoring. Experts judging A/B craft may see both versions; learners must not watch both versions of the same topic. Add a transcript/static-diagram control later to isolate the contribution of video and motion.

Why calibrate? Zheng et al. document position, verbosity and self-enhancement biases in LLM judging. Why assess learning directly? CMU’s pre/post assessment examples provide a useful starting point; the exact protocol here is our proposal.

EXISTS TODAY

packages/cli/src/render.ts, bundle.ts, ffmpeg.ts and review.ts already render movies, sample frames, inspect tagged copy and measure audio. scripts/test-narration.mjs and test-standalone.mjs exercise real speech and packaged runtime behavior.

KNOWN GAP

Current DOM review samples roughly three frames per scene and depends on tagged copy. SVG diagram labels and semantic motion can escape it. It has no corpus runner, hidden oracle, calibrated pedagogy grader or paired learner outcome. Extend the collector; do not create another media pipeline.

PROPOSED

packages/cli/src/eval/ owns Node orchestration, manifests and reports; route an eval-cli.ts sibling through the current CLI dispatch pattern. Public fixture definitions live in benchmarks/technical-video/. Sealed gold and learner answers live in a separately controlled evaluator store, not the author checkout.

SKILL LOOP

The skill writes a source/intent contract and abstraction plan before rendering. The author may critique and repair within its fixed budget. A frozen export goes to an independent evaluator. Change explainers.md for repeated planning failures; improve reusable primitives or review.ts for repeated rendering failures. Do not embed benchmark-specific scripts or gold answers in the production skill.

Every verdict identifies the exact MP4 hash, timestamp/frame interval, claim/concept ID and supporting source span. A private planning gate can terminate a failed run before expensive rendering. Sealed evaluation never returns its gold findings to the author for repair. The author’s own public-brief critic is separate. No candidate gets a silent third repair attempt.

Failure → intervention → regression

Wrong paper / scopeChange the source-resolution skill contract; add an intent trap. Do not polish the wrong film.
Detail-first / disconnectedRepair the abstraction plan and running example; require a complete early journey.
Bad arrow / clipped equationUse semantic edge or geometry evidence to identify a primitive/collector gap; keep conceptual truth in the oracle.
Voice drift / timing lossCheck the narrator lock, actual take duration, cue placement and final mux; listen to the changed spans.
Judge false approvalAdd a paired calibration defect and revise the grader separately; rerun both baseline and candidate under the new grader.

08 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

Ground the rubric, then test the rubric.

  • Existing review collector and technical explainer skill: concrete implementation anchors; the proposed suite extends these.
  • Universal Transformers revision preflight: historical scope/teaching/diagram failures from this project. Development regression evidence, not independent heldout proof.
  • Zheng et al., Judging LLM-as-a-Judge: motivates blinding, order checks and calibration; its findings do not validate a video judge automatically.
  • CMU Eberly Center, pre/post assessment examples: foundation for direct learning assessment, not a prescribed sample size or threshold.
  • NIST, paired observations: supports analyzing within-pair comparisons; the family-clustered analysis here remains a protocol to implement and validate.
  • Candidate source packs: Universal Transformers, original Raft paper, TLS 1.3 / RFC 8446. Pin file revisions/hashes during curation; do not quietly substitute newer variants.

This is a design proposal. All thresholds, schedules and corpus counts are initial choices to validate. There are no benchmark scores, learner results or claims of measured improvement yet.
